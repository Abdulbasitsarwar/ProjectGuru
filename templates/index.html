About the Scheme

Project GURU is Aston University's digital mentoring platform designed to connect students with experienced mentors from across the university. The scheme supports students in developing their academic confidence, career ambitions, and research skills.

"Mentors provide guidance, share experiences and help mentees navigate their academic journey while preparing for professional careers."

How it Works

01. Create Profile

Register and complete a mentoring profile outlining your expertise, interests, and support areas.

02. Smart Matching

Our algorithm matches participants based on domain, experience, and availability.

03. Connect

Communicate through the platform, schedule meetings, and grow together.
