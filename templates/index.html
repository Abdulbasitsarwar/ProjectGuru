About the Mentoring Scheme

Project GURU is Aston University's digital mentoring platform designed to connect students with experienced mentors from across the university. The scheme supports students in developing their academic confidence, career ambitions, research skills and personal development. Mentors provide guidance, share experiences and help mentees navigate their academic journey while preparing for professional careers.

How the Platform Works

1. Create Profile

Students and staff register on the platform and complete a mentoring profile outlining their expertise, interests and support areas.

2. Smart Matching

The system automatically matches mentors and mentees using an intelligent algorithm based on domain, experience, availability and support needs.

3. Mentoring Sessions

Once matched, participants can communicate through the platform, schedule meetings and provide feedback after sessions.

Benefits

🎓 Academic Guidance

Support with coursework, research and university life.

💼 Career Development

Mentors share industry insights and career advice.

🌍 Professional Network

Build connections with experienced academics and professionals.
